10. Assumptions

  • Chemistry: NASA CEA shifting equilibrium, infinite-area combustor, ideal expansion; real losses enter only through the c* and Cf efficiencies.
  • Thrust chamber walls are sized for hoop stress at room-temperature allowables; there is no heat-transfer or cooling analysis.
  • Feed: incompressible liquids, quasi-steady orifice and Darcy-Weisbach line flow, ideal-gas pressurant, adiabatic tanks and bottle, no start/stop transients, no acceleration head.
  • Flight: vertical 1-DOF point mass, U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976, drag from the Cd(Mach) table, no wind, no recovery phase.
